Arbiter AI: public-data pilot

On 17 November 2026, we ran Arbiter AI with a fully local Qwen3.5 model on a frozen, stratified sample of 214 public AIT-ADS alerts spanning eight simulated company networks. Arbiter escalated 72 of 86 attack-annotated alerts (83.7%), suppressed 14 attack-annotated alerts, and suppressed 5 of 128 alerts without attack annotations (3.9%). Median triage latency was 3.85 seconds on an RTX 4060 Ti desktop. These are single-pass cold-start pilot results on simulated attacks, not production detection or verified false-positive rates. Full inputs, methods, limitations, errors and results accompany the report. Dataset: Landauer, Skopik and Wurzenberger, AIT Alert Data Set, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.8263181 (CC BY 4.0).
